Initialization Protocol

SCI was established in Indonesia in 2010 to fulfill the market demands of surface preparation company in mining, oil, gas and construction.

Originated from Houston, Texas, USA, the Sodablast Systems planned expansion in Indonesia through SCI. Located in Surabaya, SCI head office provide services ranged from cleaning and paint removal, pre-coating preparation, and restoration for multiple type of surface.

DATABLOCK: BETA_2012

Industry Recognition

Within the improvement of the technology, surface preparation process requires both environmentally friendly and safety. SCI is very aware to this situation.

In January 2012, SCI was awarded the distributorship of Dustless Blasting, which is being used widely and very popular in the surface preparation industry.

DATABLOCK: GAMMA_2014

Hardware Upgrades

Following our initial success and adapting to market needs, we expanded our technological solutions.

Later in 2014, with demands of low maintenance and ease of mobility, SCI was introduced to IBIX pro series. A simple and robust Italian machine that has better safety and features than other surface preparation equipment, low cost maintenance and easy to transport.
